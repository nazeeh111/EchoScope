# Source and additions

The retained implementation comes from [computational-imaging/AcousticNLOS](https://github.com/computational-imaging/AcousticNLOS/tree/babd679b74240e8dd10fd48b3b3aa61590513c76), snapshot `babd679b74240e8dd10fd48b3b3aa61590513c76`. The source is associated with the CVPR 2019 acoustic non-line-of-sight imaging method; the named authors are David B. Lindell, Gordon Wetzstein and Vladlen Koltun.

EchoScope adds command-line dispatch repair, regression checks, documentation and repository presentation, maintained by nazeeh111. These additions do not establish new authorship of the research method or independently reproduce the source-reported scientific results.

The Stanford Computational Imaging Lab MIT notice is retained in [LICENSE](LICENSE). The separately licensed `util/pypher.py` component keeps its BSD notice. New additions use [LICENSE-branding](LICENSE-branding). The all-scenes command-line repair remains intact; the numerical reconstruction class is unchanged.
